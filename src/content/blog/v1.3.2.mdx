[The purpose of a system is what it does](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_purpose_of_a_system_is_what_it_does). A friend once mentioned in response to the idea of presentness that it's still necessary to plan for the future, or else you might as well just splurge all your money now because you're only living for the present moment. That would of course be a very unskillful reading of the literature on presentness. The purpose of your belief system is what it does to [affect your behavior](https://sive.rs/u), and if you adopt a belief system that causes you to act very unskillfully in life, then there is clearly something wrong, whether it's a gross misapplication of theory or else just strong incompatibility with this phase of your life.

Do words even [mean anything anymore](/blog/v1.1.0/)? Maybe it's easier to model perspectives as just vibes. I remember once when a friend and I were really digging a pack of fruit snacks. We bought it because it said "real fruit" somewhere, and *wow* could we taste the fruit in there. But as we munched on it, we realized it was [so suspiciously good](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Jq73GozjsuhdwMLEG/superstimuli-and-the-collapse-of-western-civilization) that we just had to double-check the packaging. And there it was, in fine print: "Real fruit sensation." Ah yes, what an impeccable sensation it was indeed, even more real of a sensation than [the real thing itself could offer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulacra_and_Simulation)! Maybe we can practice the art of sensate engineering on ourselves. Maybe we can give ourselves the real vibe sensation, spurring us to action even better than the [minimally processed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_classification) situation. To that end, I've found it useful to expand my repertoire of deployable vibes by recording them as I encounter them:

## The Present Moment

### It's just vibes

So many lenses to view the world through. What is true? What is false? The present situation, a Fourier smear across the spectrum of vibes that lies between the infinite wavelength of True and the infinite frequency of False. All narratives have something to them.[^correlation] All narratives lack nuance. Embrace the Story, then let it go: neither friend nor foe, just family at a reunion.

[^correlation]: At the very least, a narrative picks out a pattern, a correlation between theory and practice. But like Columbus sailing to Asia based on clues that landmasses lay further to the west, the correlation may be caused by far stranger continent-sized artifacts than initially presumed.

    Japan had the world of the 80's all figured out. It had stumbled on an extraordinary winning formula, one that was tailored to its unique position in global society. The 90's revealed the hollowness of its foundational economic assumptions. You can never be sure that the truth you're living out first-hand isn't resting quietly on a deeper falsehood. [Learn to stop worrying and love the revealing of a magic trick.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Strangelove)

### This is the Amos Life

Before moving to quench a desire, why not first taste that desire?[^meditation]

That taste is the taste of the Amos Life. Like stinky tofu, the pungent aroma borders on unpleasantness without actually crossing over into inedibility. It is perfectly enjoyable once you finally realize that the stinkiness is an essential part of the unique flavoring of the dish, rather than an unfortunate smell that holds the dish back from true greatness. Taste the dish fully and carefully, because it's the dish that the Divine reserved specifically for you.

[^meditation]: This is of course exactly what meditation is about in the first place anyways -- observe but do not interact with the sensations. And yet this slightly different framing of that same concept is more personally effective for me. I love tasting food, even if I'm no connoisseur and am unable to pick out the subtler undertones of flavor. I have historically been an oblivious person, at least in the visual field. Enlisting my taste buds for the synesthetic processing of life works far better than appealing to my mind's abstract observation apparatus.

### Exquisite Stress

Taste the exquisite stress of the present moment. Ahh yes, this is it. This is the archetypal "stress over checking and responding to a single simple email" experience. [Drop anchor](https://survivorsofabuserecovering.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Dropping-anchor-handout-ACE-formula-Russ-Harris-2019.pdf): this is how it feels to interact with this phenomenon in this body, inside this environment. It's a unique combination that only I will ever have the privilege of experiencing.

Wow, such pure, unadulterated stress. Fuck yeah, this is the good stuff right here. This is such an archetypal "stress over checking and responding to a single simple email" moment, it perfectly encapsulates the essence, the je ne sais quoi of the entire class of such experiences. Absolutely astounding, what a stroke of luck it is to have access to an experience of such high caliber.

### Time to make a change

It starts here. This is where it all begins. Here, right here, right now. This here is a pivotal moment where you decide to take your life in one direction instead of another.

Are you going to put this off for tomorrow? Then tomorrow will become today and you will find yourself right back here again like déjà vu. Say yes to right here, right now, and step forward into your destiny.

### Time to let go

The magic of the moment where this meets that, where internal and external realities collide to produce this mystical conflagration of qualia swirling about like a tempest in a teapot. This magic is where the meaning of life resides, imbuing the scene with a strange ink that seeps deep into the fabric of reality itself. Little vortices of significance dance and dissipate as if stirred by the swizzle stick of consciousness.

You don't exist. You never did. Let go of the sensation of control, and allow the magic of the moment to deliver its soliloquy.

### Drilling down with pinpoint precision

Just be with yourself and your life exactly the way it is right now. Exactly this amount of mind wandering during meditation, exactly this amount of lack of self-control, exactly this amount of stress, exactly this amount of deadlines, exactly this amount of self-control, exactly this amount of contentment, exactly this amount of wishing that things were different, exactly this amount of being disappointed with yourself, exactly this amount of failing to journal and capture all the notable twists and turns of your recent days, exactly this amount of recursive "exactly this amount" sensation.

## Identity

### I'm better than this

I'm better than this. Intelligence is the ability to [target the region of possible futures](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/CW6HDvodPpNe38Cry/aiming-at-the-target) one wishes to be in, and I am an intelligent person. First I had increased the range of the targeting when I stopped mistaking comfort for satisfaction and made it possible for myself to enter [previously inaccessible](/blog/v1.0.3/) states of hardship. Now I shall improve the guidance by making it possible to speed away from pathetic attractor states. I shan't mistake self-acceptance for a lack of standards, and I shan't [mistake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8SCax7RiJc) self-loathing[^changing-times] for an enemy that is to be continually snuffed out by self-love. I love my self-loathing for the destructive potential it brings, for the way it [vanquishes habits of weakness and chases them before me](https://www.goodreads.com/author/quotes/5272307.Genghis_Khan).

[^changing-times]: Just like with other instances of the ["high enabling the low"](/blog/v1.2.1/) pattern, miring myself in self-loathing was not the correct course of action earlier in my life. But things have changed now, and what was self-defeating before is now self-enabling at times. The judicious use of self-loathing can result in the skillful avoidance of personal failure states. The unskilled use of it will simply result in a degraded quality of life without any corresponding change in behavior or material circumstances.

### I'm not better than this

I'm not better than this. Internecine mental conflicts tearing my psyche apart for a chance at wrestling control over the next course of physical action. Prevailing and countervailing narratives battle it out for memetic dominance. Am I tired and in need of rest? Or is that hypothesis a mere rationalization for further procrastination?

No matter which internal faction wins, the whole facade of a unified self is shown to be a lie. The facade is not better-than; in fact, it has no authority whatsoever to dictate terms. It is nothing more than the fanciful construction of an elaborate propaganda machine designed to paint the portrait of an ego where none exists.

There is no dedicated targeting module onboard. The entirety of the mind *is* the targeting software working its own kinks out. Simply allow the process of internal error correction to take place. Any unpleasant sensations encountered are themselves [part and parcel](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/yA4gF5KrboK2m2Xu7/how-an-algorithm-feels-from-inside) of the information transfer and signaling mechanisms of the error correction machinery.

On occasion you will feel compelled to stop the process from taking place. Go along for the ride and feel the illusion of empowerment in your hands, for coups and counter-coups too are a natural part of the process.

### I'm a Loser

I'm a loser. I'm a pathetic person who can't do anything right. I'm a big pussy who doesn't dare do anything outside of his comfort zone. That's just how I am. There's got to be some people in the world who fit that description, and by golly I am one of them.

I look at my loser life and I gotta say, damn, I'm doing pretty damn well for a loser! Not many losers get to make it as far in life as I have. Yeah sure, I may suck ass at life. That's why I'm a loser, and *damn* am I fucking good at being a loser. I know all the ins and outs of living life as a loser. You ever find yourself a loser, just come find me and I'll fill you in on the loser ways. Liberate yourself from the chains of impossible expectations, and embrace your newfound sense of stunning mediocrity.

### I'm a Winner

I'm a winner. If there's one thing playing tennis in my childhood has taught me, it's that I'm a goddamn winner. I know what it's like to concentrate and get your shit together when you're down 2-5 against an opponent you *know* you can crush -- and ultimately winning the set 7-5. I know what it's like to be at the net *slamming* a volley down the empty half of the court where your opponent is not. BOOM. Point denied.

One summer when I was 12 years old, I got sent to a tennis summer camp in Beijing. There was a 16 or 17 year old Mongolian kid there -- I don't know if he was from Inner Mongolia or not, I only know that he self-identified as Mongolian. For whatever reason, this kid and I did *not* get along. At one point, he even told me, "You know I'm still underage, right? That means if I murder you, I won't have to spend the rest of my life in prison."

At the end of summer camp, I played the single highest-stakes tennis game of my life. There was no grand prize to compete for -- or if there was, I was so far removed from being a contender that it didn't register in my memory at all. But that finale between me and my Mongolian nemesis? My god, both our fragile teenage-ish egos were on the line. I held my own and edged out a motherfucking *win* like the goddamn winner I am. It was cathartic to see the coach force him to shake my hand in an acknowledgment of "Yeah I fucking beat your ass, punk."

I'm a motherfucking winner.[^nephew] I know what it's like to win, and I got that fire in me to actually seize the victory. If it looks like I'm not doing so well, that's just because I'm still warming up. Just you wait and watch. Would you bet on me? [I'd bet on me. I'd bet on me so hard.](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0102926/characters/nm0505971/) The suspense in this story isn't over whether or not I'm going to win, because I know I am. The suspense is over whether I'm going to win this very next point, and the excitement is over getting to see *how* I end up winning the whole match.

[^nephew]: I've never been more proud of my older nephew than when he beat his first chess game against me. At first it was, "Oh this situation is getting sticky, I better pay attention." Then, despite the increased attention I paid to the game, the situation morphed into "Oh no. There's just... no way out of this." No matter how much I thought, I wound up coming back to the same conclusion. Then, the inevitable end finally came.

    Ah, my nephew. He really takes after his uncle. He's every bit as much a winner as his uncle is!
